How to Place an Order

Follow the steps below to place an order from ShopZone.

1. Select your desired product.

2. Click the Add To Cart button.

3. Open the 🛒 Cart and adjust the product quantity.

4. Click the Place Order on WhatsApp button.

5. Enter your Name, Mobile Number and Address.

6. Check all information and click Send Order.

7. After receiving your order, we will contact you.
